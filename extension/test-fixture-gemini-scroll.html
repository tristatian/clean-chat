Imitates Gemini with the conversation in its own scroll box (#chat-history). Press Enter in the box: the fake site appends a question and a streaming answer, scrolls to it, rebuilds the scroll box once mid-stream, and keeps following the answer, exactly the behaviour Clean Chat has to undo.

What is PKCE and why does a static site need it?

What PKCE does

PKCE lets an app that cannot keep a secret run the OAuth authorization-code flow safely. The app proves it started the login with a one-time secret it generated itself.
